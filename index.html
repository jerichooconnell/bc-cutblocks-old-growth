Definitions. "Old growth" here uses the same threshold as the companion large-stature old-growth map: VRI stands aged >150 yr with SITE_INDEX at or above the 75th percentile for their BEC zone (–). Current cutblocks (BC's consolidated cutblocks layer, air-photo verified) are matched against the historical old-growth-capable baseline — current + stands with an explicit harvest record on equivalently high-site ground — since a logged stand's VRI age no longer shows "old". Proposed cutblocks (BC Forest Tenure cutblocks not yet disturbed and not retired, with a planned harvest date roughly within the current decade) are matched against currently standing old growth — the real question for a proposed block. A cutblock touching more than one old-growth stand is tagged with the highest site index it overlaps.
